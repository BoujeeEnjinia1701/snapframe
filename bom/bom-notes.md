# BOM notes

Costs are indicative prototype prices for one size M kit (September 2026 estimates), not quotes. Line numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`, Table 1 of SNF-PRC-001 and drawing SNF-DWG-001. Node prices come from the model volumes in SNF-CAL-001 (printed at 55 % effective fill, filament $22/kg plus $1.00 machine time per node).

- Frame kit (every line except item 11): **$431.13**, which is 7.8 % over the $400 `budget_usd`. R10 is not met.
- Tarpaulins (item 11, two at $25): $50.00, from agency stock and outside the budget (SNF-DDR-001 D1).
- All 13 lines: $481.13.
- Tube is priced per 3.05 m (10 ft) stick, one member per stick: 12 sticks of 3/4 in at $9.00 and 6 sticks of 1 in at $14.00. About 39 % of the tube bought is offcut (item O1 in SNF-DDR-001, awaiting Amish).
- SNF-CAL-001 quotes $431.09 for the frame kit; the 4 cent difference comes from rounding node prices to the cent in this BOM.
