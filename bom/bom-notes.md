# BOM notes

Costs are indicative prototype prices for one size M kit (September 2026 estimates), not quotes. Line numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`, Table 1 of SNF-PRC-001 and drawing SNF-DWG-001. Node prices come from the model volumes in SNF-CAL-001 (printed at 55 % effective fill, filament $22/kg plus $1.00 machine time per node).

- Frame kit (every line except item 11): **$443.32**, which is within the $445 `budget_usd` (approved by Amish on 2026-09-26; it was $400). R10 is met. Before SNF-DDR-002 it was $431.13; the 1 in ridge tubes add $10.00 and the single eave node variant adds about $1.76 (the corners now use the heavier four-socket node), and the larger ridge bores add about $0.47.
- Tarpaulins (item 11, two at $25): $50.00, from agency stock and outside the budget (SNF-DDR-001 D1).
- All 13 lines: $493.32.
- Tube is priced per 3.05 m (10 ft) stick, one member per stick: 10 sticks of 3/4 in at $9.00 and 8 sticks of 1 in at $14.00 (1 in rafters per SNF-DDR-001 D2 and 1 in ridge tubes per SNF-DDR-002 D8). About 39 % of the tube bought is offcut (item O1 in SNF-DDR-001, awaiting Amish).
- SNF-CAL-001 v0.2 quotes $443.30 for the frame kit; the 2 cent difference comes from rounding node prices to the cent in this BOM.
- The four push-in caps for the blank corner eave sockets are included in item 12 at no extra line cost.
