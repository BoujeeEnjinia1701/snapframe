# BOM notes

Costs are indicative prototype prices for one size M kit (September 2026 estimates), not quotes. Line numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`, Table 1 of SNF-PRC-001 and drawing SNF-DWG-001. Node prices come from the model volumes in SNF-CAL-001 v0.6: filled PA12-class nylon at 1,200 kg/m3 and 55 % effective fill, filament at $55/kg plus $1.50 machine time and hardened nozzle wear per node.

- Frame kit (every line except item 11): **$700.57**. Value-engineering target: USD 445 (`budget_usd`, a hypothetical control target, not a limit). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 700.57 (USD 255.57 over the target). SNF-CAL-001 v0.6 quotes $700.56; the 1 cent difference comes from rounding node prices to the cent here.
- Carried out on 2026-10-02 from the decisions of that day (SNF-DEC-001), the frame kit rose from $469.13 to $700.57: lines 5 to 7, the nodes, repriced for filled nylon ($13.09 a node on average, of which $0.50 is machine time; $196.42 in all); new line 15, the folding step ($25.00); new line 16, two long rear corner anchors ($12.00) with line 9 cut from eight anchors to six ($8.00 less); line 13 now four cam straps, two for each tube bundle ($26.00, was $20.00).
- Design for construction (SNF-DDR-003, 2026-10-01) had changed the frame kit from $443.32 to $469.13: new line 14, nine cable bolt sets with rings ($23.40); line 8 now has a snap hook at each end, a thimble and clips ($4.20 a cable, was $3.50); lines 5 to 7 repriced from the new node volumes (printed tabs removed, finger recesses and bolt holes added: $4.56 less in all).
- Tarpaulins (item 11, three at $25): $75.00, from agency stock and outside the frame kit (SNF-DDR-001 D1). Two cover the roof, side walls and rear gable and the third is cut for the front gable with a door flap; the cutting plan is in docs/05-build-plan.md section 3.11.
- All 16 lines: $775.57.
- Price bases: the folding step ($25.00) is an indicative retail price for a light two-step folding step of about 1.5 kg; the long anchor ($6.00) scales the $4.00 standard anchor by length (560/380 x $4.00 = $5.89); the two extra cam straps are about $3.00 each; the nylon filament price and density are indicative until a print farm quotes (open decision 11 in SNF-DEC-001).
- Tube is priced per 3.05 m (10 ft) stick, one member per stick: 10 sticks of 3/4 in at $9.00 and 8 sticks of 1 in at $14.00. About 39 % of the tube bought is offcut; accepted for the prototype on US 10 ft stock on 2026-10-02, with 1.50 m posts or 6 m metric stock chosen once the first region's tube source is known (SNF-DEC-001). The posts stay 3/4 in: the frame analysis was run (SNF-CAL-001 v0.6); 1 in posts would add about $30 (open decision 12).
- The four push-in caps for the blank corner eave sockets are included in item 12 at no extra line cost.
- Items to confirm when buying (snap button spring length, snap hook gate opening, ring and tensioner ratings, anchor eye size, the 1.5 kN rating of the long anchors, the filled nylon quote) are listed in the design decisions register, SNF-DEC-001.
- Mass: frame kit 46.2 kg (SNF-CAL-001 v0.6), in two tube bundles of 16.7 and 12.3 kg and a bag of 17.2 kg; the three tarpaulins add 13.7 kg.
